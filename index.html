Khayyam · Percussion Series

Ten percussion laboratories,
running in your browser.

Each module is a self-contained instrument built from a different piece of physics or mathematics — coupled oscillators, cymatic plates, cavitation, soliton lattices, isospectral drums, p-adic trees. Turn a knob and the underlying model responds; the sound is generated from the model, not sampled over it. No build step, no backend, no account.

Khayyam — Percussion Series · 10 modules · built for the browser

Everything runs client-side. Audio starts after your first click or key press, because browsers require a user gesture before a page may produce sound. The modules reference Tailwind, three.js and Google Fonts from public CDNs; nothing else is fetched.

Source and issue tracker: github.com/agenticocho/khayyam-percussion
