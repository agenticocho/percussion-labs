Percussion Series

Thirteen percussion laboratories,
running in your browser.

Each lab is a self-contained instrument built from a different piece of physics or mathematics — cymatics, cavitation, gongs, tuning forks, singing bowls, chimera oscillators, solitons, isospectral drums, p-adic trees, aperiodic tilings. Turn a knob and the underlying model responds; the sound is generated from the model, not sampled over it. No build step, no backend, no account. Listed in the order they were built, which tracks the order of ambition as well: technique first, then visible physics, then abstractions.

Percussion Labs · thirteen labs + two specialty pendulum pieces · built for the browser

Everything runs client-side. Audio starts after your first click or key press, because browsers require a user gesture before a page may produce sound. The modules reference Tailwind, three.js and Google Fonts from public CDNs; nothing else is fetched.
